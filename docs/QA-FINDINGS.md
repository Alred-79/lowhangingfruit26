# Care Journeys QA findings to fix (from a hands-on run on the live site)

Screenshots referenced as S/ are in this folder's shots/ directory.

## A. Must fix

A2. REQUEST STORM. Opening the chart's Care team tab, or leaving the run hub open while waiting on the student, produced thousands of `GET /api/journeys/runs/:id` per 10 s from one tab (2,188 in 10 s; later 40 in 5 s all initiated from `EventSource.onmessage`). The server backlog made an order request fail after 30 s and a patient reply hang for 4 minutes. Likely cause: `GET /api/journeys/runs/[runId]` calls `ensureProgress()` for the owner; with nothing to do the worker immediately emits an `idle` event; `useRun()` in src/components/journey/shared.tsx calls `refresh()` on every `idle`, which is another GET, which emits another `idle`. New page loads / stream reconnects replay buffered events and multiply the loop. Two tabs on one run: 75-100 requests per 10 s per tab. Required: an idle hub or tab makes about one request every few seconds at most; GET must not emit events that cause the client to GET again; replayed events must not trigger refreshes; add a client-side guard (single in-flight request, minimum interval) as a second line of defense.

A3. "Continue your visit" / "Start your visit" on the hub did nothing for 30-100 s (navigation queued behind the A2 backlog). Should be instant once A2 is fixed; make these plain links that do not wait on any request.

A4. WAITING ROOM SHOWS THE WRONG ROLE AND BRIEF. Joined as "Emergency medicine attending": the visit screen said "You are joining as the emergency medicine resident" then "You are a dietetics student conducting a telehealth nutrition assessment…" with dietetics tasks (PES statement). Joined as clinical pharmacist: "pharmacy student (clinical rotation)" then the dietetics brief. The engine now supports per-stage overrides on `cfg.journey` (see src/lib/types.ts EncounterConfig.journey): `roleTitle` (how the role is named to the student and the patient), `brief` (shown instead of the case's studentBrief), `tasks` (shown instead of the case's tasks), `patientReason` (why the patient is seeing THIS clinician today, in the patient's own words; replaces the case's opening line in the patient prompt so they stop saying "my doctor said I need to talk to a dietitian" to the ED doctor), `reason` (booked reason for this stage, shown in the chart header instead of the case's "Outpatient telehealth nutrition assessment…"), and `ai` (true for AI-clinician stages). Pass all of these from the stage definition when creating every stage encounter (student and AI). Generate brief / tasks / patientReason / reason per stage when a journey is created (the faculty guide should propose them; for the two seed journeys write them by hand in the seed data) and make them editable in the faculty journey editor. They must not give away findings.

A5. Closing the huddle left one run stuck on "Now: Writing the integrated care plan and your evaluation" for over 110 s with no spinner and no button; reload did not help; the only way out was Journey → Team huddle → "Finish writing the care plan". It must either complete or show a retry button on the banner, and survive a server restart mid-step (resume on next load).

A6. Faculty AI guide (New care journey → "Ask the guide to propose encounters") failed on first attempt with raw text "(p.stages || []).filter is not a function" (model returned stages as a string). Parse stringified arrays, retry once, and show a readable error next to the button.

A7 / D1. AI CLINICIANS ESCALATE BEYOND THE CASE. With AI playing the first stages on Walter Greene, the patient model embellished leg swelling and Dr. Lee's SBAR said "Admit for IV diuresis, telemetry… Obtain BNP, troponin" and Dr. Narang's said "I am admitting Walter urgently". The case is an outpatient telehealth visit; the student then sees him at home, and the final care plan says "Confirm whether Mr. Greene was actually admitted". AI clinician prompts (visit, note, SBAR) must state that every stage is an outpatient telehealth visit, the clinician cannot admit, order, or act outside it, may only recommend, and may only rely on facts confirmed in the shared state / ledger; assessments must not exceed that evidence. Also D2: an AI clinician told the patient "you don't have a water pill" contradicting a case fact (eplerenone, "that water pill, I forget it sometimes") and the patient retracted it — AI clinicians must not contradict the record or correct the patient's medication list from their own assumptions.

D8. AI colleagues do not know lab values that are on file: in the huddle "No information is available on … recent HbA1c", while HbA1c 9.2% is in a lab report in the chart and in the student's SBAR. The shared state holds only document titles. Record documents available at the start of a stage (and results released by actions) must enter the shared state with their content (key values from tables / report text), with provenance source 'record'.

## B. Functional

b1. Open questions are marked Resolved by a plan rather than an answer: "No recent echocardiogram is available…" shows Resolved, "Answered by: Recommend obtaining a repeat echocardiogram". A recommendation to find out is not an answer; only an entry that actually supplies the information may resolve an open question. Otherwise it stays open (optionally "plan in place").
b2. False Disputed: Marcus's whole problem list from the record shows Disputed because a clinician's remark ("atypical for the usual metabolic syndrome presentation") was linked as contradicting it; Walter's patient-reported weight loss shows Disputed though an approved result confirms it. Only a direct factual contradiction between two entries about the same thing may set Disputed; a confirmed result should make it Established.
b3. The huddle agenda (5 items) left out the most important open item on Marcus (dual beta-blocker: intentional or error?). Rank agenda by clinical importance (safety first), not recency.
b4. Care team tab (in the visit chart) does not show replies to the student's second message in a consult thread or to a whole-team call-out, although the server has them and the hub's Team messages tab shows them.
b5. Evaluation trace: "Evidence: Visit, turn 0" (there is no turn 0); a "Not credited: the evaluator described this behavior but the words it cited could not be found…" wrapper shown even when the comment itself says the behavior was not done; a real behavior (student said "you keep the dinner club", patient goals) not credited because quote matching failed; summary praised "good task division and next steps" for an SBAR with an empty recommendation. Make quote matching tolerant (normalize punctuation/case, allow a substring of the cited words), never show the "Not credited" wrapper for not-observed behaviors, and make the summary consistent with the per-behavior results.
b6. Counts disagree on the final view: header "9 uncertain or open" vs board "8 uncertain + 3 unanswered" vs evolution table "4 unanswered, 41 established vs 39 on the board". One function should compute status counts for all three.
b7. "Combines N professions" counts the team huddle as a profession and is not updated after an accepted plan change adds an owner.
b8. Consult chit-chat becomes state entries ("Perfect, I appreciate the heads-up…" stored as an Uncertain "Consult" entry with text beginning "Asked by Emergency medicine attending (…): …"). Only clinically meaningful content from consults should become entries, written as a clean statement.
b9. Name artifacts: the AI calls the student "Dr. Nursing" (demo user is "Noor Nursing"; use the student's first name, never a "Dr." title for a student); care plan says "nursing's weight trend finding" for the ED stage (use the stage role, not the student's home profession); encounter table shows "Sam Student Student".
